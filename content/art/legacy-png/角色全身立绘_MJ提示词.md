# 角色全身立绘 Midjourney 提示词

## 使用方法

1. 在 Midjourney 里上传对应头像图。
2. 复制头像图片 URL。
3. 把每条提示词开头的 `<头像图片URL>` 替换成该角色头像 URL。
4. 生成后优先选：脸最像头像、全身完整、没有多余人物、服装气质符合东北迪斯科世界观的一张。

建议统一参数：

```text
--ar 2:3 --style raw --iw 1.5
```

如果脸不像，把 `--iw 1.5` 提到 `--iw 2`。如果画面太像头像裁切，把提示词里的 `full body, head to toe, shoes visible` 放到更靠前的位置。

## 通用风格锚点

```text
full body character concept art, head to toe, shoes visible, single character, standing pose, preserve the face identity from the reference portrait, preserve age, hairstyle, facial structure and expression, expand the portrait into a complete body design, northeastern Chinese small-town winter atmosphere, post-Soviet industrial border-town mood, worn realistic clothing, painterly RPG portrait style, expressive brush strokes, muted cold colors with small warm accents, character sheet quality, plain neutral background, no text, no logo, no watermark, no cropped feet, no duplicate character
```

## 二次修图提示词

如果第一轮脸不像，用这个：

```text
same full body character, make the face closer to the reference portrait, keep the exact hairstyle, age, facial proportions, eyes, nose, mouth and expression from the reference image, do not change the outfit silhouette, no extra characters, full body, shoes visible
```

如果第一轮不是全身，用这个：

```text
zoom out to a complete full body character sheet, head to toe visible, shoes fully visible, centered standing pose, keep the same face and outfit, plain neutral background, no crop
```

如果要方便后续抠图，用这个版本：

```text
plain flat light gray background, no shadows, no environment, clean silhouette, full body character concept art, centered, generous padding around the character
```
